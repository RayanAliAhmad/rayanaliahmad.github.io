I'm Rayan A. Ali-Ahmad, a photographer, videographer, video editor and content creator based in Beirut, Lebanon. I capture streets, faces and stories through photo and film — finding warmth in every frame, from Beirut and beyond. My work has produced videos reaching over 30M views, and I've been recognized with AUB's Distinction in Student Life Award and a Video Production Award from the EAA Speak Up Competition.
